# Research basis

Research basis: PRISMA 2020 is a reporting guideline for systematic reviews (Page et al., 2021, https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71); it is not an eligibility algorithm or quality score. Cochrane Handbook guidance describes independent study selection and resolving disagreements through review procedures (https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current). Deduplication is a data-management operation and can itself require human attention; this tool groups only normalized title/DOI keys and retains each imported source record. RIS is maintained by Research Information Systems; BibTeX is a bibliographic data format. No inclusion or exclusion decision is automated.

## Limits

Title/DOI grouping can merge distinct works or miss duplicates; inspect all retained source records and resolve uncertain matches manually. The ledger records reviewer decisions but does not choose eligibility, assess bias, or adjudicate conflicts. Counts describe the records and votes in this file, not review completeness.
